# Code of Conduct

Alivyn welcomes contributors and users from different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Discuss ideas and disagreements respectfully. Critique work and behavior without attacking people. Help others understand your proposal with concrete examples and evidence. Assume good faith when possible, while taking reported harm seriously.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention without consent, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post private conversation logs, model keys, or other sensitive data, even when reporting a bug.

Maintainers may request edits, hide or remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation. Decisions will consider the behavior, its impact, and any repeated pattern. For a concern, contact the maintainer privately at [wengyanghui@foxmail.com](mailto:wengyanghui@foxmail.com). Include relevant links or screenshots and explain what happened. The maintainer will review the report privately and share an outcome with the reporter when practical.

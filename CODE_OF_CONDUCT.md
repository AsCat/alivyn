# Code of Conduct

Alivyn welcomes contributors and users from different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience. Discuss ideas and disagreements respectfully. Critique work and behavior without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe. For a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's published contact method. A direct contact address will be added before the repository is made public.
